Generative AI Declaration
=========================

The authors confirm that generative AI tools (including large language models)
were used during the preparation of this manuscript for the following limited
purposes:
  - Code scaffolding and docstring generation (reviewed and edited by authors)
  - Grammar and clarity improvements to draft text (reviewed by authors)

No AI-generated text was inserted into the manuscript without human review.
No AI tool was used to generate or fabricate experimental results, figures,
or citations. The authors take full responsibility for the integrity of all
reported results.
