Overview and Key Contributions:
Real-time conversational voice agents in transactional enterprise domains (e.g., hospitality reservations and healthcare clinic bookings) face strict constraints: responding within human conversational pauses (<800 ms time-to-first-chunk, TTFC) while simultaneously coordinating multi-system transactional workflows (CRM, vector search, calendar booking) and preserving user privacy. Conventional architectures either execute operations serially—leading to unacceptable latency spikes (>1.8 s)—or employ unconstrained heuristic parallelism that violates transactional dependencies and rate limits.

In this work, we present an end-to-end combinatorial optimization framework:
1. Offline QUBO Prompt Optimiser (QPO): Formulates prompt assembly as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization problem with token budgeting and persona-exclusivity constraints, mapped to an Ising spin-glass Hamiltonian. QPO achieves a 0% optimality gap from the brute-force optimum via simulated annealing.
2. Runtime QUBO Task Scheduler (QTP): Maps interdependent transactional workflows to a conflict-penalized QUBO model, yielding a 3.64x wave-makespan reduction (495.0 ms vs. 1800.0 ms serial) and reducing critical speech onset to 143.5 ms.
3. Full Edge-Deployable Architecture: Integrates an AudioWorklet-based client-side barge-in detector (6.4 ms interrupt latency), an in-memory BM25 retrieval engine (11.5 ms latency), role-based access control, and a zero-latency PII redaction filter. Across 300 benchmark turns in streaming mode, the pipeline delivers a median time-to-first-chunk of 323.6 ms (P90: 405.7 ms), comfortably below the conversational target.

Significance to EAAI:
This study bridges applied discrete optimization (QUBO/Ising models and quantum-inspired annealing) and conversational AI engineering, providing concrete architectural blueprints, rigorous statistical testing (bootstrap CIs, McNemar tests), and reproducible artifact releases.
